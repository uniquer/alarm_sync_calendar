Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2, 2026

At CalAlarm Sync, we take your privacy seriously. This policy outlines our commitment to your data security.

1. Data Collection

No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. This includes your name, email address, meeting titles, or attendee information.

2. Local Processing

All calendar synchronization and alarm scheduling are performed locally on your device. The app reads your calendar events strictly to calculate alarm times. This data never leaves your phone.

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not share any information with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Permissions

The app requires the READ_CALENDAR permission solely to provide its core functionality. You can revoke this permission at any time through your device settings.
